SECTION E • QUALITY ASSURANCE

Rigorous Quality Checking Parameters

In industrial mineral processing, slight variations in particle size or moisture can impair a compounding line. Deepali Minerals enforces testing across six physical and chemical parameters for all outgoing dispatches.

PARAM 01

Specific Gravity & Bulk Density

Pycnometer and tap density testing to verify material heaviness, vital for resin loading, acoustic compounding (barytes), and volumetric metering in extrusion hoppers.

PARAM 02

Whiteness & Optical Brightness

Reflectance measurement (Hunter L* / Photovolt) to ensure consistent color purity (>90% to >96% depending on mineral), critical for paint emulsions and polymer masterbatches.

PARAM 03

Moisture & Volatiles

Loss-on-drying (LOD at 105°C) verification to maintain free-flowing powder characteristics (<0.5% max for dry compounding) and prevent bubbling in thermoplastic molds.

PARAM 04

Particle Sizing & Mesh Retention

Dry and wet sieve analysis across standard industrial mesh ratings (200, 300, 400, 500 mesh) and air-classified micronized distributions (<10 to <20 microns).

PARAM 05

Chemical Assay & Purity

Quantitative titrations for primary carbonate (CaCO₃), silica (SiO₂), magnesium (MgO), and loss on ignition (LOI) to confirm technical grade specifications.

PARAM 06

Acid Insoluble Matter & Grit

Testing for non-reactive quartz grit and abrasive residues to safeguard buyer processing screws, extruder barrels, and spray nozzles from premature wear.

Technical Specification & Testing Disclaimer

Tests, reports, and guaranteed technical parameters depend strictly on the specific mineral grade, application requirements, and agreed delivery specifications. Deepali Minerals supplies verified commercial lots and does not invent unaccredited laboratory numbers or display unverified ISO certifications. Certificates of Analysis (COA) are provided for qualified commercial accounts per batch dispatch.

Deposit & Quarry Verification

We evaluate deposit consistency at source quarries in Rajasthan and Gujarat to verify raw seam quality, brightness, and chemical composition before bulk transport.

02

Pulverization & Classification

Selected raw minerals undergo crushing, pulverizing, and cyclone air-classification under strict mesh parameters to eliminate oversized grit.

03

Transit Hub Intake Inspection

Upon arrival at our Lawrence Road, New Delhi logistics facility, representative samples are inspected for moisture, whiteness, and bag seal integrity.

04

Commercial Batch Release

Approved lots are palletized and dispatched with full commercial documentation (tax invoice, e-way bill, delivery challan, and test sheet where requested).
