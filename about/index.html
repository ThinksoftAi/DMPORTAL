QUALITY VERIFICATION • DISPATCH PROTOCOLS

Quality Assurance Process & Testing Parameters

A structured 5-step operational workflow from deposit sourcing to final dispatch, coupled with systematic physical and chemical parameter checks.

01

Product Sourcing

Deposit inspection at source quarries across Rajasthan and Gujarat to verify seam purity before extraction.

02

Quality Checks

Screening for whiteness (Photovolt), particle size retention (sieve analysis), moisture, and chemical assay.

03

Product Handling

Moisture-barrier bag packaging (25/50 kg PP or 1 MT jumbo FIBC bags), palletizing, and clean lot separation.

04

Documentation

Batch Certificates of Analysis (COA), computerized GST tax invoices, and e-way bills generated promptly.

05

Enquiry & Supply

Dispatch coordination from Lawrence Road hub with local same-day delivery or scheduled pan-India freight.
